About Me

Hey! I'm Atharv Hadpe, a final-year B.E. Information Technology student at Mumbai University and a full-stack developer.

I've always been curious about computers and what happens behind the things we see on a screen. My interest in engineering actually started back in 6th standard, when I first saw code and was fascinated by the different colours and symbols on the screen.

As I grew up playing and exploring different mobile and video games, that curiosity only grew. I would often wonder how a game could run so smoothly, how people from different places could connect and play together, and what was actually happening behind the scenes.

Eventually, I realized that coding and engineering weren't limited to games. Almost everything we use on the internet is built using the same ideas, and that realization turned my curiosity into something I genuinely wanted to pursue.

Today, I enjoy building things, experimenting with new technologies, understanding how systems work, and learning something new every day. Outside of tech, I love playing and watching all kinds of sports and esports.
